## Mayan

Q'anil — Star, Seed. The Mayan day-sign is the principle you came in to embody: Q'anil (K'iche') / Lamat (Yucatec) is the seed, the spark of fertility, the multiplier. Whatever you plant grows. Whatever you commit to has the principle of cumulative abundance attached. The shadow of Q'anil is over-fertility — too many seeds in too many fields, none of them given the duration to mature. The medicine is selective fidelity: plant fewer things, water them longer.

Tone 3 — the Activator, the Spark. The thirteen tones are a vertical scale of intention; Tone 3 is the moment the seed makes contact with the soil. It is the spark of motion, the first stroke, the friction that lights. People with Tone 3 are often the catalyst — they bring the energy that gets something started, even when they don't carry it through. The shadow of Tone 3 is restlessness: moving on before the seed has rooted.

The trecena starts at Kame / Cimi — the Death trecena. The 13-day arc that contains Q'anil 3 is governed by transformation, by the necessity of letting one form die so the next can be born. The Death-led trecena teaches that fertility is not the opposite of death — it is what follows death, what comes through it. Your seed-energy is not naive. It knows what it costs.

Long Count 12.18.2.16.8 — you were born deep into the close of the thirteenth bak'tun, in the period the Maya marked as the great transition. The cosmological location of your birth is the threshold itself.
