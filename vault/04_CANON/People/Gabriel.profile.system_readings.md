## Mayan

K'at — the Net. The Mayan day-sign K'at (K'iche') / Kan (Yucatec) is the principle of binding, gathering, weaving disparate threads into a single fabric. The Net is what captures abundance and what entangles — the same structure does both. Whatever you bring close to you stays close; the gift is the holding capacity. The shadow of K'at is over-binding — too many threads gathered, the weaver tangled in their own weave. The medicine is selective gathering: know which threads belong to the cloth and let the others pass through.

The shadow of Tone 8 is over-harmonizing: smoothing what should stay rough, integrating what should stay separate. The medicine is to let the contrast hold.

The trecena starts at No'j / Caban — the Earth / Wisdom trecena. The 13-day arc that contains K'at 8 is governed by accumulated knowing, by the deep memory of place and pattern. The Wisdom-led trecena teaches that what gathers must be informed by what has been learned; the net is woven from the lessons of the soil. Your weaving-energy is not blind. It carries the memory of the earth it grew from.

Long Count 12.18.16.16.4 — you were born late in the thirteenth bak'tun, well into the great-closing period the Maya tracked as the threshold.
