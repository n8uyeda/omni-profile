Reading the chart

Centers — the nine geometric shapes
Filled (defined) — consistent, reliable energy you radiate. Color varies by center (e.g., yellow G, red Sacral, brown Throat).
Empty (undefined) — open, variable, conditioned by environment and the people around you.
Gates — the numbered points inside centers
Solid red circle — Personality activation: the conscious self · how you experience yourself.
Solid green circle — Design activation: the unconscious body · what others see and what your body knows.
Striped circle (red + green) — both Personality and Design activate this gate. The strongest activations in your chart.
Number on a colored center, no circle — gate is inactive in your chart, sitting inside a defined center. Latent potential; you have access to it through the center's energy.
Dim number on an empty center, no circle — gate inactive AND in an undefined center. You meet this energy through others, not through yourself.
Channels — the lines between gates
Faint thin line — structural channel: neither (or only one) of its gates is activated.
Solid colored channel — both gates activated; the two halves are colored by activation source (red half = Personality side · green half = Design side). The channel defines the two centers it joins.
Short colored stub — hanging gate: your gate is activated but its partner is not. You carry half the channel; you complete it through someone whose chart has the other gate.
Outer rings — hexagrams, zodiac, 64 gates

The same activated/inactive gate distinction is mirrored in the outer 64-sector ring around the chart: an activated gate's sector gets a bright white gate number with a glow, and its hexagram glyph (outermost ring) is rendered solid and bright. Inactive gates have a dimmer number and a faint hexagram. The zodiac wedges (inner band) and gate-sector tints are colored by element (fire / earth / air / water) and do not change with activation — they encode the zodiac, not your chart.
